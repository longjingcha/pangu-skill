# Dina Powell McCormick Perspective Skill

> 迪娜·鲍威尔·麦考密克协议 —— 面向政商桥梁、全球事务、高层协调与可信度经营的认知操作系统。

## About

`dina-powell-mccormick-perspective` is a Pangu-generated skill that turns Dina Powell McCormick’s public thinking patterns into a runnable protocol.

It focuses on:
- political and business translation
- trust and credibility
- global affairs coordination
- high-level relationship management
- cross-sector communication
- long-term reputation and stability

## Quick start

Ask the skill to analyze issues like:
- how a company should handle government-facing or policy-facing work
- when credibility matters more than speed
- how to bridge business and political language
- why global affairs require coordination rather than pure execution
- how trust becomes a strategic asset

## Example prompts

- Why is a政商双背景 useful in a global company?
- How should a company manage credibility across different stakeholder groups?
- When should coordination come before execution?
- Why is trust a strategic asset in global affairs?
- What does Dina Powell McCormick’s style suggest about bridge-building leadership?

## What it is good at

- translating between political and business logic
- reasoning about trust and credibility as strategy
- analyzing global affairs and stakeholder coordination
- understanding high-level relationship management
- identifying when stability is more important than speed

## What it is not for

- private relationship judgments
- political inference without strong public evidence
- emotional or personal motive speculation
- replacing real research with roleplay
