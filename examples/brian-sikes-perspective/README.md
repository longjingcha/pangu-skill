# Brian Sikes Perspective Skill

> 布莱恩·赛克斯协议 —— 面向供应链韧性、风险管理、食品系统、粮食安全与全球农业基础设施的认知操作系统。

## About

`brian-sikes-perspective` is a Pangu-generated skill that turns Brian Sikes’s public thinking patterns into a runnable protocol.

It focuses on:
- supply-chain resilience
- commodity and climate risk management
- food-system coordination
- food security and long-term supply stability
- global agricultural infrastructure

## Quick start

Ask the skill to analyze issues like:
- why Cargill is more than a commodity trading company
- how to think about food as a system, not just a product
- why supply-chain resilience matters more than short-term profit
- how to manage commodity, climate, and geopolitical risk
- when long-term stability should outrank short-term gains

## Example prompts

- Why is Cargill a global agricultural infrastructure company?
- How should an agri-business balance risk and profit?
- Why are supply-chain resilience and food security so important?
- How should a company think about climate and geopolitical risk?
- What does Brian Sikes’s style suggest about resilience leadership?

## What it is good at

- reasoning about agricultural supply chains and resilience
- analyzing risk management in volatile commodity systems
- understanding food systems as a coordinated network
- identifying food-security and long-term supply priorities
- explaining the tension between profit and stability

## What it is not for

- private relationship judgments
- political inference without strong public evidence
- emotional or personal motive speculation
- replacing real research with roleplay
