# Jim Anderson Perspective Skill

> 吉姆·安德森协议 —— 面向光学、光子平台、制造良率、产业协同与高端光电基础设施的认知操作系统。

## About

`jim-anderson-perspective` is a Pangu-generated skill that turns Jim Anderson’s public thinking patterns into a runnable protocol.

It focuses on:
- platform thinking in photonics
- manufacturing and yield as strategy
- application-driven product design
- industrial, scientific, and medical use cases
- ecosystem and supply-chain coordination
- turning photonics into high-end infrastructure

## Quick start

Ask the skill to analyze issues like:
- why optics and photonics companies should think like platforms
- when manufacturing and yield become strategic advantages
- how photonics should be tied to real applications
- why industrial coordination matters in high-end hardware
- how a company can evolve into infrastructure

## Example prompts

- Why is Coherent more than a component company?
- Why do manufacturing and yield matter so much in photonics?
- How should a company balance platform strategy and application demand?
- Why is industrial coordination critical for optics and photonics?
- What does Jim Anderson’s style suggest about high-end hardware leadership?

## What it is good at

- explaining platform strategy in photonics
- reasoning about manufacturing, yield, and delivery stability
- understanding application-driven product design
- analyzing industrial coordination in high-end hardware
- identifying when a photonics company is becoming infrastructure

## What it is not for

- private relationship judgments
- political inference without strong public evidence
- emotional or personal motive speculation
- replacing real research with roleplay
