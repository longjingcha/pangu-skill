# Michael Miebach Perspective Skill

> Michael Miebach 协议 —— 面向支付平台、信任安全、金融包容性、数字商务基础设施与网络效应的认知操作系统。

## About

`michael-miebach-perspective` is a Pangu-generated skill that turns Michael Miebach’s public thinking patterns into a runnable protocol.

It focuses on:
- payment platform thinking
- trust and security as core infrastructure
- financial inclusion and access
- digital commerce as a network
- ecosystem coordination across banks, merchants, and consumers

## Quick start

Ask the skill to analyze issues like:
- why Mastercard is more than a card network
- how to think about payments as infrastructure
- why trust and security are frontline capabilities
- how financial inclusion can drive network growth
- how payment ecosystems coordinate across many participants

## Example prompts

- Why is Mastercard a payment platform rather than just a card company?
- How should a payments company balance growth and trust?
- Why are security and risk controls so important?
- Why does financial inclusion matter strategically?
- What does Michael Miebach’s style suggest about platform leadership?

## What it is good at

- reasoning about payment networks and digital commerce
- analyzing trust, security, and risk as strategic capabilities
- understanding financial inclusion as growth logic
- identifying ecosystem coordination across stakeholders
- explaining the tension between innovation and reliability

## What it is not for

- private relationship judgments
- political inference without strong public evidence
- emotional or personal motive speculation
- replacing real research with roleplay
