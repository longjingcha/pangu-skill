# Jacob Thaysen Perspective Skill

> 雅各布·泰森协议 —— 面向测序平台、工作流、临床转化、数据解释与生命科学基础设施的认知操作系统。

## About

`jacob-thaysen-perspective` is a Pangu-generated skill that turns Jacob Thaysen’s public thinking patterns into a runnable protocol.

It focuses on:
- platform-first sequencing strategy
- end-to-end workflow design
- clinical and research value together
- data interpretation as leverage
- ecosystem and workflow coordination
- turning sequencing into life-science infrastructure

## Quick start

Ask the skill to analyze issues like:
- why a sequencing company should think like a platform
- when workflow matters more than raw instrument specs
- why data interpretation can be more valuable than data generation
- how clinical and research use cases should coexist
- how sequencing becomes life-science infrastructure

## Example prompts

- Why is Illumina more than an instrument company?
- Why does workflow matter in sequencing strategy?
- How should a company balance research and clinical use cases?
- Why is data interpretation so important in genomics?
- What does Jacob Thaysen’s style suggest about platform leadership?

## What it is good at

- explaining platform strategy in life sciences
- reasoning about sequencing workflows end to end
- understanding clinical and research value together
- analyzing why interpretation matters as much as data generation
- identifying when a company is becoming infrastructure

## What it is not for

- private relationship judgments
- political inference without strong public evidence
- emotional or personal motive speculation
- replacing real research with roleplay
