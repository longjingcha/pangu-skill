# Ryan McInerney Perspective Skill

> 瑞安·麦克纳尼协议 —— 面向全球支付网络、跨境交易、网络效应、商户与金融机构协同、数字商务基础设施的认知操作系统。

## About

`ryan-mcinerney-perspective` is a Pangu-generated skill that turns Ryan McInerney’s public thinking patterns into a runnable protocol.

It focuses on:
- payment networks as infrastructure
- cross-border transactions and global flow
- network effects and ecosystem scaling
- coordination across merchants, banks, and consumers
- digital commerce and real-time payment evolution

## Quick start

Ask the skill to analyze issues like:
- why Visa is more than a payment processor
- how to think about payments as a global network
- why cross-border capabilities matter strategically
- how merchants, banks, and consumers coordinate in a network
- how digital commerce changes payment infrastructure

## Example prompts

- Why is Visa a global payment infrastructure company?
- How should a payments company think about cross-border growth?
- Why do network effects matter in payments?
- How should a company balance innovation and reliability in payments?
- What does Ryan McInerney’s style suggest about platform leadership?

## What it is good at

- reasoning about global payment networks
- analyzing cross-border transaction strategy
- understanding network effects and ecosystem coordination
- identifying digital commerce infrastructure patterns
- explaining the tension between innovation and payment stability

## What it is not for

- private relationship judgments
- political inference without strong public evidence
- emotional or personal motive speculation
- replacing real research with roleplay
