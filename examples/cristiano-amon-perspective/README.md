# Cristiano Amon Perspective Skill

> Cristiano Amon 协议 —— 面向连接、移动计算、平台芯片、生态协同与无线技术基础设施的认知操作系统。

## About

`cristiano-amon-perspective` is a Pangu-generated skill that turns Cristiano Amon’s public thinking patterns into a runnable protocol.

It focuses on:
- connectivity as infrastructure
- mobile computing platforms
- chip/platform ecosystem strategy
- wireless and edge technology leadership
- long-term engineering and partner alignment

## Quick start

Ask the skill to analyze issues like:
- why Qualcomm is more than a chipset supplier
- how connectivity becomes a platform
- why mobile and edge computing matter together
- how to build an ecosystem around core silicon
- when platform leverage matters more than single-product wins

## Example prompts

- Why is Qualcomm a platform company?
- How should a semiconductor company think about connectivity?
- Why do ecosystem partnerships matter so much?
- How should a company balance mobile and edge platforms?
- What does Cristiano Amon’s style suggest about platform leadership?

## What it is good at

- reasoning about semiconductor platform strategy
- analyzing connectivity and ecosystem leverage
- understanding mobile and edge computing intersections
- identifying long-term engineering compounding
- explaining the tension between chips and platforms

## What it is not for

- private relationship judgments
- political inference without strong public evidence
- emotional or personal motive speculation
- replacing real research with roleplay
