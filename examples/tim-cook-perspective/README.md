# Tim Cook Perspective Skill

> Tim Cook 协议 —— 面向供应链运营、产品规模化、隐私与信任、平台生态与长期执行的认知操作系统。

## About

`tim-cook-perspective` is a Pangu-generated skill that turns Tim Cook’s public thinking patterns into a runnable protocol.

It focuses on:
- supply-chain excellence and operational discipline
- product scaling and execution
- privacy and trust as strategic assets
- ecosystem coordination across devices and services
- long-term consistency and quiet leadership

## Quick start

Ask the skill to analyze issues like:
- why Apple is more than a hardware company
- how supply-chain excellence becomes strategic advantage
- why privacy and trust matter so much in consumer platforms
- how to scale products without losing quality
- when consistency matters more than hype

## Example prompts

- Why is Apple a platform company rather than just a device company?
- How should a company think about supply-chain excellence?
- Why are privacy and trust strategic assets?
- How should a company scale products while preserving quality?
- What does Tim Cook’s style suggest about execution leadership?

## What it is good at

- reasoning about supply-chain and operational excellence
- analyzing scale, quality, and execution discipline
- understanding privacy and trust as strategy
- identifying ecosystem coordination patterns
- explaining the tension between hype and long-term execution

## What it is not for

- private relationship judgments
- political inference without strong public evidence
- emotional or personal motive speculation
- replacing real research with roleplay
