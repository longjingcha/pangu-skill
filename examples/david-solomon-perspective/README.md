# David Solomon Perspective Skill

> 苏德巍协议 —— 面向市场周期、机构客户、执行力、品牌治理与平台协同的认知操作系统。

## About

`david-solomon-perspective` is a Pangu-generated skill that turns David Solomon’s public thinking patterns into a runnable protocol.

It focuses on:
- market-cycle awareness
- execution quality and speed
- institutional client relationships
- brand and culture governance
- platform coordination across business lines

## Quick start

Ask the skill to analyze issues like:
- why markets and timing matter in investment banking
- how execution quality shapes credibility
- why institutional clients are so important
- when brand and culture become strategic assets
- how to balance business-line coordination with speed

## Example prompts

- Why is Goldman Sachs more than a deal-making shop?
- How should a bank balance market opportunity and execution quality?
- Why are institutional client relationships so important?
- Why do brand and culture matter in investment banking?
- What does David Solomon’s style suggest about market leadership?

## What it is good at

- reasoning about market cycles and execution windows
- analyzing institutional client dynamics
- understanding brand, culture, and governance as strategy
- identifying platform coordination across business lines
- explaining the tension between speed and internal balance

## What it is not for

- private relationship judgments
- political inference without strong public evidence
- emotional or personal motive speculation
- replacing real research with roleplay
