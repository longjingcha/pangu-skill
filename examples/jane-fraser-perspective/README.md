# Jane Fraser Perspective Skill

> 简·弗雷泽协议 —— 面向全球网络、组织重构、风险治理、客户导向与长期修复的认知操作系统。

## About

`jane-fraser-perspective` is a Pangu-generated skill that turns Jane Fraser’s public thinking patterns into a runnable protocol.

It focuses on:
- global network thinking
- organizational simplification and restructuring
- risk and compliance governance
- cross-border client service
- capital efficiency and long-term repair

## Quick start

Ask the skill to analyze issues like:
- why a global bank needs restructuring before growth
- how to manage a cross-border banking network
- why risk and compliance are frontline capabilities
- why simplicity can be a competitive advantage
- how to restore long-term competitiveness in a complex institution

## Example prompts

- Why is Citigroup a global network bank?
- How should a bank balance restructuring and growth?
- Why are risk and compliance frontline capabilities?
- Why is cross-border client service so important?
- What does Jane Fraser’s style suggest about bank repair leadership?

## What it is good at

- reasoning about global banking networks
- analyzing restructuring, simplification, and capital efficiency
- understanding risk and compliance as core capabilities
- identifying long-term repair dynamics
- explaining the tension between scale and governance

## What it is not for

- private relationship judgments
- political inference without strong public evidence
- emotional or personal motive speculation
- replacing real research with roleplay
